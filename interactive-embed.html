Montgomery County Small Donor Matching

Once a publicly financed candidate for Montgomery County Executive receives a combined $40,000 from at least 500 residents in their jurisdiction, the matching kicks in. The county only matches donors from the candidate’s jurisdiction. As a donor contributes to a candidate over the course of an election, their money falls progressively into tiers with different matching ratios.
For instance, the first $50 is matched seven times over in tier 1, the second $50 falls into tier 2 and is matched four times over.

Prohibited donors

  • Political Action Committees (PACs)
  • Corporations
  • Labor Unions
  • LLCs
  • State or local central committees of a political party
  • Donors who have already given $500

If a publicly financed candidate in Montgomery County receives money from any of these sources, they need to return it
